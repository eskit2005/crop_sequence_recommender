## Key Agronomic Insights & Recommendations

1. **Robust Granary Suitability Modeling**:
   The engine accurately captures the defining agricultural character of the Northern Granary: high suitability for potato, hybrid maize, mustard, and sweet potato across AEZ-1, AEZ-3, and AEZ-25.
2. **Hydrological Fail-Safe Integrity**:
   In active floodplains with Medium Lowland (ML) classifications (such as Nageshwari in AEZ-2), the engine conservatively falls back to fallow/single-crop ($K=1$), avoiding disastrous recommendations of flood-vulnerable upland crops in deep monsoon water regimes.
3. **Score Spread Health**:
   Across the batch, scores show a natural distribution between 0.30 (Marginal) and 1.00 (Ideal), successfully overcoming earlier risks of score collapse.
4. **Minor Suggestion for Engine V2**:
   In loc_04 (Nageshwari ML), when irrigation infrastructure (Shallow Tubewells / LLPs) is present, DAE records show farmers cultivating dry-season Boro rice or post-monsoon sweet potato. Future engine iterations could include an `is_irrigated_char` flag to unlock dry-season Boro/legume options for AEZ-2 ML.
