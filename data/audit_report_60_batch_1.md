## 2. Audit Methodology & Scientific Validation Criteria

Each of the 15 locations was evaluated using a 4-tier validation rubric grounded in peer-reviewed agronomy, field trial bulletins from the Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI), Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI), Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC), and Upazila Agricultural Offices of the Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE):

1. **Hydrological & Topographical Realism ($K$ Fidelity):**
   - High Land (HL) & Medium High Land (MHL): Above normal inundation level during monsoon season. Farmers can cultivate year-round ($K=3$) using minor irrigation. Cropping intensity across northern floodplains typically spans 210% to 260%.
   - Medium Low Land (ML) Active Riverine Chars: Flooded up to 90–180 cm during the monsoon (July–October). Soil is dynamic coarse-to-fine sand/silt. Cropping intensity is strictly limited by flood hazards and winter residual moisture constraints ($K=2$).
2. **Seasonal Crop Realism:**
   - Rabi (Winter: Nov–Feb): Temperature drops (10–25°C), low precipitation, dependence on residual moisture or irrigation.
   - Kharif-1 (Pre-Monsoon: Mar–Jun): High heat, pre-monsoon showers, suitable for deep-rooting fiber crops (Jute/Kenaf) or early cereals (Aus/Maize).
   - Kharif-2 (Monsoon: Jul–Oct): Heavy rains, flooded soils, staple Transplanted Aman (T. Aman) in non-inundated lands.
3. **Score Calibration & Tier Separation:**
   - Ideal Tier: 0.80 to 1.00 (Highly suitable, optimal agro-climatic match, extensive local adoption).
   - Suitable Tier: 0.55 to 0.79 (Moderate suitability, secondary crop, soil or moisture constraints).
   - Variance Verification: The model must not collapse into homogeneous 1.00 values.
4. **Authentic Ground Truthing:**
   - Web searches against DAE annual production reports, BARI On-Farm Research Division (OFRD) multi-crop trials, and BRRI cropping system surveys.
